Why this system is different

Every answer passes a controlled assurance pipeline.

  1. 01
    Retrieve approved knowledge

    Only active, date-valid knowledge is considered.

  2. 02
    Apply critical-topic thresholds

    Fees, dates and policies require stronger evidence.

  3. 03
    Clarify or escalate safely

    Uncertain questions become reviewable knowledge gaps.

  4. 04
    Test after improvement

    Regression questions verify that updates fixed the problem.

CAPSTONE PROJECT

One focused, credible system

Built as a student-level implementation of an enterprise RAG customer-support platform.

PROBLEM

School information changes, but chatbots do not know what is current.

Parents receive generic, incomplete or outdated answers, while administrators have no structured way to identify and repair knowledge failures.

SOLUTION

Verified answers plus a controlled improvement loop.

SchoolTrust checks sources, dates and confidence, escalates uncertainty, records knowledge gaps and retests answers after administrator-approved updates.
